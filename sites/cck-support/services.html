Flexible care at home, from 30 minutes to 24 hours

Our visits normally run between 8am and 8pm, and we offer a 24 hour service when you need it. Every plan is built around you and reviewed as your needs change.

Personal care & medication

Help with the everyday, done your way

We are registered with the Care Quality Commission for personal care. Our carers help with the things that keep you comfortable, healthy and feeling like yourself.

  • Washing, bathing and showering
  • Toileting and continence aids
  • Dressing and undressing
  • Getting up and going to bed
  • Skin, hair, teeth and nail care
  • Changing stoma bags
  • Collecting medication
  • Help taking medication
Home help & companionship

A helping hand and a friendly face

Sometimes the most important part of a visit is the chat. Our carers help keep your home running and make sure you stay connected to the people and places you love.

  • Preparing and serving meals
  • Shopping and putting it away
  • Washing up, laundry and ironing
  • Gardening and general errands
  • Sit in service and call in visits
  • Trips out and social events
  • Getting to medical appointments
  • Help with bills and finances

Experienced support for complex needs

Alongside everyday home care, we offer mental health support work and specialist brain injury support, plus rehabilitation, supported living and community based services.

Respite & family relief

Time for you, peace of mind for them

Looking after someone you love is hard work. Our caregiver relief service gives family carers time to rest, run errands or simply have a break, knowing their loved one is in safe and caring hands.

We also help after a stay in hospital, so the move home goes smoothly.
