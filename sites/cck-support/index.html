Getting started

Three simple steps to care at home

Give us a call

Ring the office on 01227 668 041 or send us a message. We will listen, answer your questions and talk you through your options.

Free home visit

Our Registered Manager visits you at home, or in hospital, for about an hour to understand your needs. There is no charge for this.

Meet your carers

We match you with carers who suit you, introduce them first, and you only start paying when your care begins.

Why families choose us

A small local team that knows you by name

CCK Support was started in 2012 by five carers who knew care could be done better. We are still local, still family run, and still hands on.

  • Your own Key Worker. One main contact who gets to know you, your history and how you like things done.
  • Introduced before they visit. You always meet a carer before they come alone. Never a strange face at the door.
  • Trained and checked. Every carer is DBS checked, has two references, carries photo ID and is trained before their first visit.
  • Trusted by the NHS and council. Many of our clients come to us through local authority and NHS referrals.
