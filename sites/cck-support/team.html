Our carers are the heart of everything we do

We look for people with patience, understanding and a genuine love of helping others. Then we train them well and match them carefully with the people they support.

Our story

Started by carers, still run by carers

Five of us had worked together at a previous company and felt we could do better. In 2012 we started CCK Support. It took about a year to build up our first clients, and the trust of the local authorities and the NHS came with it.

Today we support people from young adults to the elderly, many referred to us by the council and the NHS. Our Registered Manager, Susanne Wild, still knows every client and every carer personally.

The Key Worker system

One person who really knows you

Every client can choose a Key Worker. They are your main point of contact and your voice within our team.

Gets to know you

Your history, your family, your hobbies, the food you like and how you like things done all go into your care plan.

Keeps your plan right

Your Key Worker arranges regular reviews with you and your family, and updates your plan as your needs change.

Always your choice

Not everyone gets on. If you would like a different Key Worker, just tell us. It will be sorted kindly and quietly.

Safe hands

Checked, trained and always learning

Before any carer visits on their own, they are DBS checked, we take up two references, and they carry a CCK photo ID card.

Training before the first visit

  • Full induction programme
  • Basic first aid
  • Safe handling of medication
  • Safeguarding vulnerable adults
  • Moving and handling
  • Infection control, food hygiene, health and safety
  • Mental Capacity Act and confidentiality

Ongoing courses

  • Dementia care
  • Palliative care awareness
  • Brain and head injury awareness
  • Diabetes awareness
  • Drug and alcohol awareness
  • Challenging behaviour and risk assessment
  • NVQs and diplomas in care
In loving memory

Kate Arnold, 1973 to 2018

Kate was one of our founders and became one of our Registered Managers. She was the people's person, always hands on, always fair, and always going above and beyond for the people we support.

Kate passed away from leukaemia in 2018. In her memory, our team raises money for local respite care and the blood cancer charity Bloodwise, through events like our charity mud run and boot fairs.
