The brief

A personal brand for a DJ and rave night, Rave with Jacob. It has to feel loud, dark and hand-made, read instantly on a flyer or a 16 px tab, and stand apart from generic EDM neon.

Final design

Vinyl J

The J's hook is half a spinning record: one groove, one spindle.

  • A letter mark that belongs to Jacob, not to a genre
  • Vinyl is implied by a single groove, not drawn literally
  • Acid green on night black reads like a club light, yet works in pure black
  • Small-size cut drops the groove so the favicon stays crisp
